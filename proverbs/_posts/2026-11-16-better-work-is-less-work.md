When you are overwhelmed with too much work, think of a way to do less of it, in better ways. 

The version of the work that gets better also gets smaller. The edit that helps is almost always a cut: a feature, a pass, a paragraph. [Anything added dilutes everything else](https://www.joshbeckman.org/proverbs/anything-added-dilutes-everything-else) and [Before making it better, stop making it worse](https://www.joshbeckman.org/proverbs/before-making-it-better-stop-making-it-worse).

This does not reverse [The reward for good work is more work](https://www.joshbeckman.org/proverbs/the-reward-for-good-work-is-more-work).
