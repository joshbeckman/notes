Two things I’m thinking about this. One is about what level of granularity you should have agents watching the system. You can’t watch at a super high product level, but watching at a super minute level is too costly and too prone to misreads. 

The second is that we should be making bespoke harnesses for things like this. I think the generalized harness will fall away. ￼
